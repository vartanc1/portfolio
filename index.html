Jonathan Valvano's Embedded Systems course, UT Austin

Bare-metal two-wheel robot

A real-time robot written in bare-metal C on an ARM Cortex-M4 with no hardware abstraction libraries. GPIO, timers, PWM, and UART are configured directly at the register level, with an ultrasonic distance-sensing and motor-control loop and live UART telemetry for debugging.
